Complete the five-year financial forecast in the provided DCF model workbook.

The workbook contains a discounted cash-flow valuation model for an equity investment.  Historical data for 2023 (column E) and a set of key assumptions (growth rates, margins, tax rate, WACC, terminal-growth assumptions) are already populated.  Your job is to build out the five-year forecast period (2024-2028, columns F-J) across the Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash-Flow Statement, and Calculation sheets, and to fill the DCF column-E valuation block (rows 4-50, excluding the pinned assumption rows).

### Scope of work

1. **Income Statement (sheet `IS`)** – Populate rows 4-43, columns F-J with revenue, COGS, operating expenses, EBIT, tax, and net-income forecasts derived from the assumptions and the prior-year carry-forward logic used in the model.

2. **Balance Sheet (sheet `BS`)** – Populate rows 4-74, columns F-J with asset, liability, and equity projections.  Rolling balances and depreciation schedules must follow the conventions already visible in the historical columns.

3. **Cash-Flow Statement (sheet `CFS`)** – Populate rows 4-34, columns F-J with operating, investing, and financing cash flows.  Free cash flow to the firm is the intermediate used by the DCF engine.

4. **Calculation sheet (sheet `Calcs`)** – Populate rows 4-75, columns F-J (intermediate schedules, depreciation, capex, and working-capital schedules) except rows 10, 26, 45, 53, and 57, which contain model parameters that should not be overwritten.

5. **DCF valuation column (sheet `DCF`, column E, rows 4-50)** – Complete the discount-cash-flow arithmetic: FCFF per period, cumulative discount factors, present values, terminal value, and equity-value-per-share.  Rows 15, 31-33, 37-40, and 42-44 contain inputs / assumptions that should be preserved.

All formulas must be internally consistent across sheets.  Preserve the existing formatting conventions, currency signs, and decimal precision.

Save the completed workbook as `/app/output.xlsx`.
