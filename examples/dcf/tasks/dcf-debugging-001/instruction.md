# Audit & Correction: Aster Vale Products DCF Model

You are provided with a financial model for **Aster Vale Products** (fictional), a three-statement forecast linked to a DCF valuation.

The model contains errors in its calculation formulas that affect the forecast projections and/or the valuation output. Your task is to:

1. **Audit** the workbook to identify all formula errors.
2. **Correct** every error so that all calculations produce accurate results consistent with the model's own assumptions and business notes on the *Key Assumptions* sheet.
3. **Preserve** the supplied assumptions, workbook structure, sheet names, and layout. Do not modify assumption values, add new sheets, or rearrange existing content.
4. Save the corrected workbook as `/app/output.xlsx`.

The model spans interconnected sheets (*Key Assumptions*, *IS*, *BS*, *CFS*, *Calcs*, *DCF*). Cross-check the internal logic: balance-sheet identities, cash-flow roll-forwards, asset-schedule depreciation/amortization mechanics, and the DCF valuation chain. Focus on whether each formula implements the business rules stated in the model's own documentation.
