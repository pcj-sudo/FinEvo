Aster Vale Products — DCF Valuation Model
=========================================

This workbook contains a multi-sheet financial model for Aster Vale Products, a fictional manufacturing company. It includes:

- **IS**: Income statement (2020–2027)
- **BS**: Balance sheet (2020–2027)
- **CFS**: Cash flow statement (2020–2027)
- **Calcs**: Calculation schedules (PP&E, intangibles, debt, equity roll-forwards)
- **Key Assumptions**: Forecast assumptions, business rules, and policy descriptions
- **DCF**: Discounted cash flow valuation, WACC, and sensitivity analysis

All monetary values are in USD millions, unless otherwise noted.
Business rules and forecast methodology are documented in the *Key Assumptions* sheet.
