Complete the financial forecast for Aster Vale Products through 2027.

The workbook contains an integrated DCF valuation model with linked income
statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement. Historical data covers
2020–2022; the 2023–2027 forecast period is incomplete.

Build the forecast using the assumptions and conventions documented in the
workbook. The model spans six worksheets (DCF, IS, BS, CFS, Key Assumptions,
Calcs) with cross-sheet dependencies. Complete depreciation schedules, debt
roll-forwards, working capital, cash flows, and the valuation bridge.

Preserve all existing formulas and conventions. Save the completed workbook
as `/app/output.xlsx`.
