Financial Model Workbook
========================

This workbook contains a multi-year quarterly financial model for
Aster Vale Systems, a semiconductor company with September fiscal year-end.

Sheets:
- Business Definitions: Entity description and accounting conventions
- Income Statement: GAAP and non-GAAP income statement with tax bridge
- Balance Sheet: Assets, liabilities, equity, and key metrics
- Cash Flow: Statement of cash flows (operating, investing, financing)
- Segments: Revenue and margin breakdown by business segment
- Operating Drivers: Assumptions, growth rates, margins, tax rates
- Opening Balances: Starting balances as at 30 September 2010
