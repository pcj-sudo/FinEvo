# Financial Model Audit – Quarterly Consolidated Statements

You are provided with a quarterly financial model for a technology company covering three consecutive quarters. The workbook contains an Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement, Segment analysis, Operating Drivers, and opening balance assumptions.

## Task

Audit the model and correct all calculation errors. Preserve the supplied assumptions (Operating Drivers, Opening Balances, Business Definitions) and the overall workbook structure. The corrected workbook must maintain internal consistency across all statements:

- Balance Sheet must balance (total assets = total liabilities + equity).
- Cash Flow must reconcile to the change in cash on the Balance Sheet.
- Retained earnings on the Balance Sheet must roll forward correctly from the Income Statement.
- Key cross-statement links (interest income, amortization, share-based compensation, debt schedule) must be consistent.

Save the corrected workbook as `/app/output.xlsx`.
