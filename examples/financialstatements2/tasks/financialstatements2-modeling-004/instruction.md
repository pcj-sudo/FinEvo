Complete the quarterly financial forecast model for Aster Vale Technologies
for fiscal years 2025E and 2026E using the supplied assumptions in the Drivers sheet.

The workbook contains an Income Statement (IS), Balance Sheet (BS), and Cash Flow
Statement (CF) with quarterly granularity. Historical actuals (FY2001A-FY2021A) and
earlier forecasts (FY2022E-FY2024E) are already populated with working formulas.
The forecast columns for FY2025E and FY2026E (columns DS through EB) have been
cleared and require you to reconstruct the full model across all three statements.

Use the Drivers sheet for all operating assumptions, including revenue forecasts by
product segment, gross margins, operating expense ratios, tax rates, working capital
days, depreciation and amortization rates, capital expenditures, share repurchase
policies, dividend assumptions, and financing parameters. The Opening sheet provides
the balance sheet opening positions. The Debt Terms sheet defines outstanding debt
instruments.

Key metrics to compute include: revenue by segment, gross profit, operating
expenses, net income, EPS, balance sheet roll-forwards (assets, liabilities, equity),
and cash flow statement (operating, investing, financing). Ensure the balance sheet
balances (Total Assets = Total Liabilities + Equity) and the cash flow statement
reconciles to the balance sheet cash balance.

Preserve the existing conventions: column layout, row labels, fiscal-year-end dates,
and the quarterly-then-annual structure (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4, Annual per fiscal year).
