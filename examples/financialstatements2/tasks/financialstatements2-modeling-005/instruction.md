Complete the financial forecast for Aster Vale Technologies for fiscal years FY2022 through FY2026.

The workbook contains actual historical data through FY2021 (fiscal year ends June) across three statements: Income Statement (IS), Balance Sheet (BS), and Cash Flow Statement (CF). The Drivers sheet provides growth rates, margin assumptions, tax rates, and other forecasting parameters for each quarter.

Using the provided drivers, assumptions, and historical context in the workbook, build a complete quarterly forecast model. Populate all forecast cells (marked Q1E-Q4E in the column headers) across the Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement. The model should:

- Decompose revenue by segment using the growth rates in the Drivers sheet
- Compute gross margins, operating expenses, and net income based on provided rates
- Roll forward balance sheet items from opening balances using income statement and cash flow results
- Maintain cross-statement consistency (balance sheet must balance; cash must tie)

Reference the Deep Dive section at the bottom of the IS sheet for the detailed segment breakdown and computation structure. The Drivers sheet contains per-quarter parameters including revenue growth rates by product family, gross margin assumptions, operating expense rates, tax rates, and interest rate assumptions.

Preserve the existing conventions, units (USD million), and structure of the workbook. Save the completed workbook as `/app/output.xlsx`.
